## 19.1 — What support hours are offered?

Standard support operates Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 US Eastern time, excluding published holidays. Enterprise has continuous paging for Severity 1 service outages. Routine configuration questions submitted overnight wait for the standard support window.

## 19.2 — How is a Severity 1 incident defined?

Severity 1 means the production service is unavailable to most users of an affected tenant with no reasonable workaround. A single-user permission issue or a request for a new feature is not Severity 1. Support can reassess severity as impact changes.

## 19.3 — What initial response targets apply to support cases?

Enterprise targets a 30-minute initial response for Severity 1 and four business hours for Severity 2. These are acknowledgement targets, not resolution-time guarantees. Standard-plan cases target one business day for an initial response.

## 19.4 — How is planned maintenance communicated?

Planned customer-impacting maintenance is normally announced at least seven days in advance on the status page. Emergency security maintenance may occur sooner. The maintenance window and any service-credit treatment are governed by the applicable subscription terms.

## 19.5 — Can customers request an architecture or security review call?

Enterprise prospects and customers can request a scoped review with Security or Architecture through their account team. Requests for internal diagrams, test results or supplier reports may require an NDA and redaction. A review call does not authorize new product commitments.
