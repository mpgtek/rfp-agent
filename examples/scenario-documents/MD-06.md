## 6.1 — In which regions can customer content be hosted?

At tenant creation, customers select either the US region in Virginia or the EU region in Frankfurt. Case records, attachments, search indexes and backups stay in the selected region. The service does not offer a Singapore or Australia hosting region.

## 6.2 — Can an existing tenant change its hosting region?

A region change requires a support-led migration to a new tenant and a planned cutover. There is no in-place region toggle. The migration requires approval of the new region, export/import validation and deletion scheduling for the original tenant.

## 6.3 — Can support personnel access EU tenant content from outside the EU?

Yes, in limited circumstances. Approved support staff may access tenant content through audited, time-limited sessions from the US or EU. Selecting EU storage does not promise EU-only personnel access; customers must review the documented transfer safeguards.

## 6.4 — Is a data processing addendum available?

A standard data processing addendum is available for enterprise review. It describes processor obligations, subprocessors and international transfer terms. Customer-specific changes require privacy and legal review; sales staff cannot approve modified clauses.

## 6.5 — How are data subject requests handled?

The customer acts as controller and authenticates the requestor. MeridianDesk provides export and deletion tools for customer administrators, and Privacy can assist through a verified support case. Legal preservation requirements can limit deletion of specific records.
