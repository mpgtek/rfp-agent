## 20.1 — How are user licenses counted?

Subscriptions count named active Agent and Administrator seats at the billing snapshot. Read-only Auditors are included up to the plan limit of 25. Shared logins are prohibited, and disabling a user affects the next billing snapshot rather than retroactively changing prior invoices.

## 20.2 — Are data export fees charged at termination?

A standard self-service tenant export is included during the 30-day post-termination retrieval window. Assisted transformations, repeated custom exports or third-party migration work may require a separate statement of work. No unlimited professional-services commitment is included.

## 20.3 — Can customers negotiate a shorter breach notification deadline?

A requested notification deadline that differs from the standard terms requires Legal and Security review. Account teams cannot accept a one-hour deadline or guarantee notice before an incident is verified. The signed agreement determines any customer-specific commitment.

## 20.4 — Does the standard plan include a dedicated technical account manager?

No. Enterprise can purchase a named technical account manager as an additional service with defined meeting cadence. The standard support queue remains the incident reporting path; a named contact is not a replacement for the on-call escalation process.

## 20.5 — Will MeridianDesk accept unlimited liability for data incidents?

No standard offer includes unlimited liability. Liability caps, indemnities and any higher data-protection cap require Legal approval in the signed agreement. A questionnaire response cannot amend those terms or create an insurance commitment.
