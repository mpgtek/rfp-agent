# MD-02 — Assurance operating guide (SIMULATED)

SIMULATED INTERNAL EVIDENCE. All facts describe an invented company and have no external attestation.

Product: MeridianDesk Enterprise SaaS

Owner: GRC manager

Version: 2026.10-demo

Reviewed: 2026-10-29

## 2.1 — Is a SOC 2 Type II report available?

In this fictional scenario, a SOC 2 Type II report covering Security and Availability for January through June 2026 is available under NDA. It covers the production SaaS service, not customer-managed integrations or beta features. This is a simulated report, not an actual attestation.

## 2.2 — Is MeridianDesk ISO 27001 certified?

No. The fictional company has not obtained ISO 27001 certification. An internal gap assessment exists, but neither that assessment nor a sales proposal should be represented as a certificate.

## 2.3 — What independent penetration testing is performed?

An external assessor tests the web application and public API annually and after a major authentication redesign. Customers can request a redacted executive summary and remediation status under NDA; raw exploit details are restricted.

## 2.4 — May customers store payment card data in the product?

No. Payment card numbers and card verification codes are prohibited in case records and attachments. Subscription payments are collected by an external payment processor through its hosted payment page; MeridianDesk does not offer a PCI card-data environment.

## 2.5 — Will MeridianDesk sign a HIPAA business associate agreement?

No. MeridianDesk does not offer a business associate agreement and is not intended for protected health information. Customers requiring that agreement must select another service rather than treating standard privacy terms as a substitute.
