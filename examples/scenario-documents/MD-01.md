# MD-01 — Governance operating guide (SIMULATED)

SIMULATED INTERNAL EVIDENCE. All facts describe an invented company and have no external attestation.

Product: MeridianDesk Enterprise SaaS

Version: 2026.10-demo

Reviewed: 2026-10-29

## 1.1 — Who is accountable for the information security program?

The Security Lead owns the program and reports risk decisions to the COO monthly. Engineering owns remediation delivery; Product cannot waive a high-risk finding without Security Lead approval.

## 1.2 — How often are security policies reviewed?

Policy owners review the security policy set annually and after a material architecture or regulatory change. The policy register records the owner, revision date and approval decision.

## 1.3 — How are security exceptions approved and tracked?

Exceptions require a named business owner, compensating controls and a Security Lead approval. They expire after at most 90 days; renewal requires a fresh risk assessment.

## 1.4 — How is the risk register maintained?

Security and service owners assess risks quarterly using likelihood and impact ratings. High risks receive a remediation owner and target date, and overdue items are escalated to the COO.

## 1.5 — What customer responsibilities apply to MeridianDesk?

Customers administer their users, configure identity-provider policies, classify uploaded information and review tenant permissions. MeridianDesk operates the application, underlying infrastructure and backups; customers remain responsible for their own exports.
