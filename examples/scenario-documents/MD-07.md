## 7.1 — How long are deleted case records retained before purge?

Deleted case records remain recoverable in a tenant recycle bin for 30 days, then are purged from active databases during a daily job. The retention period is fixed in the current product; backups can retain prior copies for up to 35 additional days.

## 7.2 — What is the backup retention period?

Encrypted daily backups are retained for 35 days in the tenant's selected region. They are used for disaster recovery and cannot be searched or selectively edited as a customer archive. Expired backup objects are removed through storage lifecycle rules.

## 7.3 — What happens to customer data after subscription termination?

An authorized tenant owner can export data during a 30-day retrieval window after termination. Active content is then scheduled for deletion; backup copies age out within the normal 35-day backup cycle. Legal holds are handled separately by Legal.

## 7.4 — Can customers configure a legal hold on individual cases?

No native per-case legal hold exists. Customers with preservation obligations should export records into their own controlled archive before normal deletion occurs. A support request is not a substitute for a formal preservation arrangement.

## 7.5 — How are storage media disposed of?

Cloud infrastructure providers handle physical media retirement under their documented disposal controls. MeridianDesk removes logical objects and destroys obsolete access paths; it does not physically handle disks or provide a customer-specific disk destruction certificate.
