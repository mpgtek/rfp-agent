## 14.1 — What background screening applies to employees?

Background screening is performed before workforce access is granted where permitted by local law. The scope depends on role and jurisdiction. A delayed or legally prohibited check requires People Operations and Security to agree on access restrictions.

## 14.2 — What security training do employees receive?

Employees complete security and privacy training during onboarding and annually thereafter. Engineers also complete secure-development training. Completion is tracked, and overdue training can result in suspension of privileged access until it is completed.

## 14.3 — How are employee devices secured?

Workforce laptops are centrally managed, use full-disk encryption and endpoint detection, and lock after at most ten minutes of inactivity. Devices with disabled management or overdue critical updates are blocked from the workforce access gateway.

## 14.4 — Are personal devices allowed to administer production?

No. Production administrative access requires a managed corporate laptop with a valid device posture check. Personal phones may be used as an MFA factor, but that does not authorize production access from a personal computer.

## 14.5 — How quickly is access removed when an employee leaves?

People Operations schedules account disablement for the employee's effective departure time. Identity, access-gateway and source-control access are revoked through the offboarding workflow, with completion checked by Security. Involuntary departures are coordinated before notice is delivered.
