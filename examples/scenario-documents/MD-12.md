## 12.1 — Is there a documented incident response process?

The response process covers triage, containment, evidence preservation, recovery and post-incident review. An incident commander coordinates Security, Engineering and Customer Support, with Legal and Privacy engaged when the incident affects personal information.

## 12.2 — When are customers notified of a confirmed data breach?

The standard data processing terms require notice without undue delay after confirmation of a breach affecting that customer's personal data, with an initial target of 48 hours after confirmation. This is not a promise to report every unverified alert or to accept a customer's proposed one-hour deadline.

## 12.3 — How often is the incident response plan exercised?

Security runs a cross-functional tabletop exercise twice per year, including one customer-data exposure scenario. The exercise records response gaps, accountable owners and due dates. Exercise completion is tracked separately from recovery testing.

## 12.4 — How are customers informed during a service incident?

The public status page provides incident updates and tenants can subscribe to email notifications. For confirmed tenant-specific security incidents, Support contacts the registered security contact through an authenticated case; sensitive details are not published on the public page.

## 12.5 — What post-incident information is shared with affected customers?

For a material outage or confirmed customer-data incident, MeridianDesk prepares a written summary describing impact, timeline, contributing causes and corrective actions. Distribution may be limited to affected customers, and details that expose another tenant or an active investigation are redacted.
