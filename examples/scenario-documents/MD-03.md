## 3.1 — Which single sign-on protocols are supported?

Enterprise tenants can use SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect with one customer identity provider. Starter tenants use email-based accounts. Identity-provider initiated SAML is supported; multiple simultaneous identity providers per tenant are not.

## 3.2 — How is multi-factor authentication enforced?

Workforce access requires phishing-resistant security keys. Enterprise customer tenants enforce MFA through their identity provider. Local customer accounts can enable authenticator-app MFA, but tenant-wide mandatory local MFA is not available on Starter.

## 3.3 — What is the password policy for local accounts?

Local passwords must have at least 14 characters and are checked against a breached-password denylist. Passwords are salted and hashed with Argon2id; arbitrary periodic password rotation is not required unless compromise is suspected.

## 3.4 — How are users provisioned and deprovisioned?

Enterprise tenants can create, update and disable users using SCIM 2.0. Disabling a user revokes active application sessions within 15 minutes. Starter tenants manage users manually; disabling an identity-provider account alone does not remove historical audit records.

## 3.5 — What session timeout controls are available?

Interactive sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity and have a 12-hour absolute lifetime. Enterprise administrators may shorten the inactivity timeout to 15 minutes. API tokens follow their own expiry settings and do not inherit browser session timeouts.
